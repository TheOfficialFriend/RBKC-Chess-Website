About Us

Chess Without Boundaries

RBKC Chess Community isn't an officially consolidated chess club — it's a constellation of people and their will to play chess, established in 2024. Our mission is to promote chess education and create inclusive spaces where people of all ages and skill levels can enjoy the game.

From complete beginners to experienced players, everyone is welcome at the board.

Leonel MbahoChess Coach & Founder
